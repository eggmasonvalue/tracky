Remaining, when a warranty replacement arrives:
- Capture one handle weigh-in and check per-segment Z20 ≥ Z100, especially the trunk (`trunk_order` in the sync/MCP output).
- If the trunk is ordered (`wire`), the transposition never triggers; consider whether to keep the rule.
- If the replacement is also inverted, it is a model-wide firmware ordering; record that in PROTOCOL.md and keep the rule.
- Err2 on every handle weigh-in should disappear on a healthy unit.
